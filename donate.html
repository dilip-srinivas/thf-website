Frequently Asked Questions on 80G Donations

How do I claim my 50% tax deduction under Section 80G?

Under Income Tax rules introduced via Form 10BD/10BE, The Heritage Foundation files an annual statutory return of donations with the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT). Once filed, your contribution automatically reflects in your Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Form 26AS. We also email you the certified Form 10BE certificate bearing the Income Tax portal unique reference number.

Why is a PAN Card mandatory for donations?

The Income Tax Department mandates that every donor claiming Section 80G tax deductions must provide their 10-digit PAN. Without a valid PAN, the tax authorities reject Form 10BE generation and tax deduction benefits cannot be claimed.

Can Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) or Foreign Citizens donate?

Non-Resident Indians holding an Indian passport and an Indian PAN can contribute strictly from their domestic resident savings or current bank accounts in Indian Rupees (INR). However, to protect our institutional neutrality, we do not accept funds from foreign passports, overseas credit cards, foreign foundations, or foreign currency remittance (FCRA).
