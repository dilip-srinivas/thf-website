Legal Evidentiary Standards

How to Capture Actionable Ground Evidence

Before an administrative authority or high court bench can issue an emergency stop-work order, the facts must be incontrovertible. If it is safe to do so, document:

1. GPS Geotagged Media

Enable GPS location on your smartphone camera so latitude/longitude coordinates and UTC timestamps are hardcoded into the EXIF photo metadata.

2. Land Identifiers (Survey No.)

Note the Revenue Village name and, if known, the Survey Number (S.No.). If unknown, provide a distinct permanent landmark or nearest milestone.

3. Machinery Identification

For mini-excavators or trucks, photograph the registration number plate, engine tonnage classification (e.g. Kubota U-15/U-35), and operator trailer details.
